# Simple Interest Calculator

## Introduction
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple tool used to calculate the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specific period of time at a given rate of interest.

## Formula
Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:
- P = Principal Amount
- R = Rate of Interest per year
- T = Time in years

## Example
Suppose:
- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate of Interest = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Calculation:
SI = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100
SI = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
Total Amount = ₹11,000

## Features
- Calculates simple interest easily.
- Uses principal, rate, and time as inputs.
- Displays the interest amount.
- Calculates the total amount.
